Thesis
Norric AB builds a real-time statistical model of commercial Sweden. Each operating product (Kreditvakt for corporate insolvency, Sigvik for the BRF housing stock, SIGNAL for municipal procurement) ingests data directly from authoritative Swedish primary sources and contributes a calibrated signal to a shared intelligence core.

The operating thesis is that in the AI era, intelligence is abundant but three things remain scarce: elapsed time spent ingesting, regulatory permission to ingest, and institutional trust in what is published. Norric optimises for all three.
