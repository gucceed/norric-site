Agent-facing surface of the rail: 42 tools over Streamable HTTP. Discovery is free and anonymous - initialize, tools/list, status and freshness, no account needed. Paid calls quote exact prices over x402 and settle in the same session: swedish_company_verify_v1 at $0.002 is the trust anchor, swedish_company_changes_v1 at $0.02 is the changes feed. The rail · what Norric is becoming
📇
Official registers only
Every record comes from a national company register: Bolagsverket in Sweden, CVR in Denmark, Brønnøysund in Norway, PRH in Finland. No scraped guesses, no resold third-party data. Provenance an auditor can follow back to the source.
💳
Per-call, paid by the caller
No seats, no contracts, no invoices. An agent pays for exactly the call it makes over x402: $0.002 to verify a company, $0.02 for the changes feed. The cheap verify call is the free sample; the feed is the subscription.
🔌
Zero onboarding
Machine-readable from the first byte: manifest, OpenAPI, llms.txt, free tools/list. An agent discovers, pays and gets an answer in one session. No account, no API key, no human in the loop.
🇪🇺
EU-resident end to end
Official EU sources, production database in Stockholm, compute and payment facilitator in Amsterdam. The compliance story a US data API cannot tell.
🧭
Machine trust by default
Freshness is queryable, coverage is stated per tool, and failure is honest: found=false instead of a guess. That is what gets an agent to call twice.
🏛️
Built for the operator too
The human who funds the wallet gets what compliance needs: official sources only, per-call metering, and an audit trail on every score.
Thesis
Norric is becoming the rail agents use to check European companies. Each operating product (Kreditvakt for corporate insolvency, Sigvik for the BRF housing stock, SIGNAL for municipal procurement) ingests from official primary sources and feeds the same pipes the rail sells by the call.

The bet is simple: agents do not read marketing, they read manifests, and their eval loops punish anything that guesses. So the rail earns trust the machine way - official registers only, freshness you can query, honest failures, provenance on every response. Snapshots do not compound; history and subscriptions do. Four countries are wired into production now, and every country added makes the rail harder to route around.
